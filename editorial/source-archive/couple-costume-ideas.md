The best couple Halloween costumes are the ones that tell a story. Each costume should be recognizable on its own, but together they create something more interesting than either one alone.

The ideas below are organized from classic to punny. Choose the pairing that reflects your actual relationship.

## Classic and Timeless

### 1. Gomez and Morticia Addams

Gomez in a pinstripe suit with slicked-back hair and a thin mustache; Morticia in a long black dress with pale skin and dark lips. This is the most iconic couple costume and suits a couple with a dramatic, romantic dynamic.

The thin mustache is the most important element for Gomez.

### 2. Sandy and Danny (Grease)

Sandy in a poodle skirt and a white blouse, Danny in a leather jacket and jeans. Or both in the final scene outfits — Sandy in black leather, Danny in a letterman jacket.

The final scene outfits are more recognizable than the opening scene ones.

### 3. Bonnie and Clyde

1930s outfits — a beret, a midi skirt, and a toy gun for Bonnie; a suit, a flat cap, and a toy gun for Clyde. This is a classic, stylish couple costume.

The flat cap and the beret are the two most important elements.

### 4. Romeo and Juliet

Romeo in a Renaissance doublet and tights; Juliet in a Renaissance gown with a flower crown. This is a romantic, classic couple costume that suits a couple who wants to look beautiful.

The flower crown is the most important element for Juliet.

### 5. Frankenstein and His Bride

Frankenstein in a dark suit with green face paint and bolts; the Bride in a white dress with a tall, streaked wig. This is a classic Halloween couple costume with a clear narrative.

The tall, streaked wig is the most important element for the Bride.

## Pop Culture

### 6. Mia and Vincent (Pulp Fiction)

Mia in a white shirt, black trousers, and a black bob wig; Vincent in a black suit with a ponytail. This is a stylish, recognizable pop culture couple costume.

The black bob wig is the most important element for Mia.

### 7. Jack and Sally (The Nightmare Before Christmas)

Jack in a black pinstripe suit with a skull face; Sally in a patchwork dress with stitching makeup. This is a romantic, gothic couple costume that suits a couple with a dark aesthetic.

The patchwork dress is the most important element for Sally.

### 8. Shrek and Fiona

Shrek in a green bodysuit or green face paint with brown vest and boots; Fiona in a green dress with a red braid. This is a funny, recognizable couple costume.

The green face paint is the most important element for both characters.

### 9. Tony Stark and Pepper Potts

Tony in a suit with an arc reactor prop; Pepper in a business outfit with a Stark Industries badge. This is a stylish, recognizable couple costume that suits a couple who wants to look good.

The arc reactor prop is the most important element for Tony.

### 10. Eleven and Mike (Stranger Things)

Eleven in a pink dress with a blonde wig and a bloody nose; Mike in a 1980s outfit with a walkie-talkie. This is a recognizable pop culture couple costume.

The bloody nose makeup is the most important element for Eleven.

### 11. Wednesday and Thing (Addams Family)

Wednesday in a black dress with braids; the partner dressed as Thing — a disembodied hand in a white glove. This is a funny, unexpected couple costume that suits a couple with a sense of humor.

The white glove is the most important element for Thing.

### 12. Barbie and Ken

Barbie in a pink outfit with a blonde wig; Ken in a pink outfit with a "Ken" name tag. This is a topical, funny couple costume that suits a couple with a sense of humor about gender expectations.

The more pink, the better.

## Punny Couples

### 13. Salt and Pepper

One person in a white outfit labeled "Salt," the other in a black outfit labeled "Pepper." This is the most classic punny couple costume and always gets a reaction.

Use large, readable labels — the pun needs to be visible from across the room.

### 14. Plug and Socket

One person dressed as an electrical plug, the other as a socket. This is a clever, slightly cheeky punny couple costume.

The props are the most important elements — make them large and readable.

### 15. Peanut Butter and Jelly

One person in a brown outfit labeled "Peanut Butter," the other in a purple outfit labeled "Jelly." This is a cheerful, recognizable punny couple costume.

Use large, readable labels — the pun needs to be visible from across the room.

### 16. Devil and Angel

One person in a red outfit with horns and a tail; the other in a white outfit with wings and a halo. This is the most classic couple costume and suits a couple with contrasting personalities.

The horns and the halo are the two most important elements.

### 17. Ketchup and Mustard

One person in a red outfit labeled "Ketchup," the other in a yellow outfit labeled "Mustard." This is a simple, recognizable punny couple costume.

Use large, readable labels — the pun needs to be visible from across the room.

### 18. Netflix and Chill

One person in a Netflix-branded outfit; the other in a blanket and pajamas. This is a funny, relatable couple costume that suits a couple with a sense of humor about their relationship.

The Netflix logo needs to be large and clearly readable.

## Elegant and Sophisticated

### 19. Dracula and His Bride

Dracula in a black tuxedo with a cape, pale makeup, and fangs; the Bride in a white dress with pale makeup and a dramatic updo. This is an elegant, classic couple costume.

The cape is the most important element for Dracula.

### 20. Phantom of the Opera and Christine

The Phantom in a black suit with a white half-mask and a cape; Christine in a white dress with a flower in her hair. This is a romantic, theatrical couple costume.

The white half-mask is the most important element for the Phantom.

### 21. Cleopatra and Mark Antony

Cleopatra in a white or gold dress with a black wig and dramatic eye makeup; Mark Antony in a Roman military outfit. This is a classic, stylish couple costume.

The dramatic eye makeup is the most important element for Cleopatra.

### 22. Day of the Dead Couple

Both partners in elegant outfits with Day of the Dead skull makeup — intricate floral patterns in black and white with color accents. This is a beautiful, striking couple costume that suits a couple who enjoys elaborate makeup.

The skull makeup is the most important element — invest time in making it look beautiful rather than scary.

## Final Thoughts

The best couple Halloween costumes are the ones that reflect the actual relationship. Choose a pairing you are both genuinely excited about, coordinate the execution level, and commit to the characters for the whole evening. A couple costume that tells a story is always more memorable than two unrelated costumes worn by people who happen to be standing together.
