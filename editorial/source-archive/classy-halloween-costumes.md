A classy Halloween costume is one that makes people say "you look amazing" before they say "what are you?" The ideas below are chosen for their elegance, their wearability, and their potential to look genuinely beautiful rather than just recognizable.

Quality materials and specific character references are what distinguish a classy costume from a cheap one.

## Gothic Glamour

### 1. Morticia Addams

A long, form-fitting black dress, pale skin, dark lips, and long dark hair worn loose. Morticia is the gold standard of elegant Halloween costumes — she is instantly recognizable, inherently beautiful, and requires no props.

A long, form-fitting black dress is the most important piece. Everything else follows from it.

### 2. Elvira

A long black dress with a plunging neckline, big black hair, and dramatic makeup. Elvira is a classic Halloween character whose look is inherently glamorous.

A black wig with volume is the most important piece.

### 3. The Bride of Frankenstein

A white dress with a tall, streaked wig, pale makeup, and dramatic eye makeup. The Bride's look is theatrical and striking — it photographs beautifully.

The tall, streaked wig is the most important piece.

### 4. A Victorian Ghost

A white or ivory Victorian dress, pale makeup, dark circles under the eyes, and an ethereal expression. This is a beautiful, atmospheric costume that suits a woman who wants to look haunting rather than scary.

A genuine vintage or vintage-inspired dress elevates this costume significantly.

### 5. The Black Swan

A black tutu or a black dress with feather details, dramatic black and white makeup, and a feather headpiece. This is a striking, theatrical costume inspired by the film.

The dramatic makeup — black and white with red accents — is the most important element.

## Timeless Characters

### 6. Audrey Hepburn in Breakfast at Tiffany's

A little black dress, long gloves, a pearl necklace, an updo, and a cigarette holder prop. This is one of the most elegant Halloween costumes — it is beautiful, recognizable, and requires no face paint or prosthetics.

The updo and the long gloves are the two most important elements.

### 7. Cleopatra

A white or gold dress, a black wig with a blunt fringe, a gold headpiece, and dramatic eye makeup. Cleopatra is a timeless Halloween character whose look is inherently regal.

The dramatic eye makeup — thick black liner and gold eyeshadow — is the most important element.

### 8. Marie Antoinette

A pastel ball gown, a powdered wig, beauty marks, and an elaborate updo. Marie Antoinette's look is theatrical and opulent — it is one of the most visually striking Halloween costumes.

The powdered wig and the pastel ball gown are the two most important elements.

### 9. Josephine Baker

A 1920s-inspired outfit — a beaded dress, a feather boa, a headband with a feather, and a confident expression. Josephine Baker's look is glamorous and distinctive.

The feather boa and the beaded dress are the two most important elements.

### 10. Frida Kahlo

A colorful embroidered blouse, a long skirt, flowers in the hair, and drawn-on connected eyebrows. Frida Kahlo's look is artistic and distinctive — it is one of the most recognizable and most beautiful Halloween costumes.

The flowers in the hair and the connected eyebrows are the two most important elements.

## Elegant Villains

### 11. Maleficent

A black dress, dramatic horns, pale makeup, dark lips, and dark hair. Maleficent's look is inherently elegant — she is a villain who takes her appearance seriously.

The horns are the most important piece — choose a quality pair that stays on securely.

### 12. Cruella de Vil

A black and white outfit, dramatic makeup, and a fur coat or stole. The key is the hair — half black, half white. This is a striking, theatrical costume.

A dramatic smoky eye and a bold red lip complete the look.

### 13. The Evil Queen (Snow White)

A black and purple gown, a crown, pale makeup, dark lips, and dark hair. The Evil Queen's look is regal and theatrical.

The crown and the dark lips are the two most important elements.

### 14. Ursula (The Little Mermaid)

A black and purple dress, dramatic makeup, white hair, and tentacle details. Ursula is a theatrical villain whose look is inherently dramatic.

The white hair and the dramatic makeup are the two most important elements.

### 15. Lady Tremaine (Cinderella)

A grey or purple gown, grey hair in an elegant updo, and a cold, imperious expression. Lady Tremaine's look is sophisticated and understated — she is a villain who relies on elegance rather than theatrics.

The grey updo and the cold expression are the two most important elements.

## Mythological and Historical

### 16. Medusa

A green dress, snakes woven into the hair, and dramatic makeup. Medusa is a mythological character whose look is inherently striking.

Fake snakes woven into a bun or a braid create the most convincing Medusa hair.

### 17. Athena

A white or gold dress, a helmet or a laurel wreath, a shield prop, and a wise, composed expression. Athena is a mythological character whose look is inherently regal.

The helmet or laurel wreath is the most important element.

### 18. The Phantom's Christine (Phantom of the Opera)

A white dress with a flower in the hair, pale makeup, and an expression of wonder. Christine's look is romantic and ethereal.

The white dress and the flower in the hair are the two most important elements.

### 19. Titania (A Midsummer Night's Dream)

A flowing, ethereal dress in white or pale green, a flower crown, and fairy wings. Titania is a literary character whose look is inherently beautiful.

The flower crown and the flowing dress are the two most important elements.

### 20. The Lady of Shalott

A white dress, long flowing hair, and a slightly tragic expression. The Lady of Shalott is a Pre-Raphaelite literary character whose look is hauntingly beautiful.

A genuine white or ivory dress elevates this costume significantly.

## Final Thoughts

The best classy Halloween costumes are the ones that make you feel beautiful and confident. Choose a character whose aesthetic you genuinely admire, invest in one quality piece — a dress, a wig, a piece of jewelry — and focus on the makeup and hair. A costume that makes you feel good is always the right choice.
