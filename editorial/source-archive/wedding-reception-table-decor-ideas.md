Wedding reception table decor creates the atmosphere that guests will remember long after the food is forgotten. A beautifully set table — with the right centerpiece, warm candlelight, and personal touches at each place — makes every guest feel genuinely welcomed and celebrated.

The ideas below cover every element of wedding reception table decor, from the runner to the favour.

## 1. Choose the Centerpiece Height First

The most important decision for wedding reception table decor is the centerpiece height. Low arrangements — under 30cm — allow guests to see and talk across the table. Tall arrangements — over 60cm — allow guests to see under them.

Mid-height arrangements between 30cm and 60cm block sightlines and make conversation difficult. Avoid them.

## 2. The Table Runner

A table runner sets the tone for the whole table. Choose a runner that suits the wedding aesthetic — linen for a relaxed look, satin for a formal look, or a floral print for a garden party feel.

Use the same runner across all tables for a cohesive look.

## 3. Candles on Every Table

Candles create the warmth that makes a wedding reception feel genuinely romantic. Use a mix of pillar candles and taper candles at varying heights on every table.

Place them in stable holders and keep them away from overhanging fabric and flowers.

## 4. The Place Setting

A charger plate under the dinner plate, a folded napkin on top, and a name card and menu at each place creates a layered, considered place setting.

Use the same place setting across all tables for a cohesive look.

## 5. The Name Card

A name card at each place setting makes guests feel individually welcomed and removes the awkward moment of everyone deciding where to sit.

Use a consistent design across all name cards — the same font, the same card stock, and the same style.

## 6. The Menu Card

A menu card at each place setting lets guests know what to expect and adds a personal touch. Use the same design as the name cards for a cohesive look.

## 7. The Favour

A small favour at each place setting — a personalised ornament, a small candle, or a homemade food gift — doubles as a decoration and a thank-you gift.

## 8. Greenery on the Table

Loose sprigs of eucalyptus, ferns, or ivy scattered along the table among the centerpiece and candles add a natural, lush quality to the table.

## 9. The Charger Plate

A charger plate — a decorative plate placed under the dinner plate — adds a layer of texture and colour to the place setting. Use charger plates in the same finish across all tables.

## 10. The Napkin Fold

A simply folded napkin — a rectangle or a bishop's hat — looks more considered than elaborate napkin origami. Tie with a ribbon or a sprig of greenery for a finishing touch.

## Final Thoughts

Wedding reception table decor is most effective when every element — runner, centerpiece, place settings, and candles — works together as a cohesive whole. Start with the centerpiece height decision, add the runner and candles, set the places, and finish with personal touches. The result should look abundant, warm, and genuinely welcoming.
