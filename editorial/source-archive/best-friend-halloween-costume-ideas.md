The best duo Halloween costumes are the ones that are incomplete without the other person. Each costume should be recognizable on its own, but together they tell a story.

The ideas below are organized from classic to punny. Choose the pairing that reflects your actual friendship.

## Classic Duos

### 1. Thelma and Louise

Two women in 1990s road trip outfits — jeans, a bandana, sunglasses, and a determined expression. This is a classic, empowering duo costume that suits two women with a strong friendship.

The bandana and the sunglasses are the two most important elements.

### 2. Sandy and Danny (Grease)

Sandy in a poodle skirt and a white blouse, Danny in a leather jacket and jeans. Or both in the final scene outfits — Sandy in black leather, Danny in a letterman jacket.

The final scene outfits are more recognizable than the opening scene ones.

### 3. Bonnie and Clyde

1930s outfits — a beret, a midi skirt, and a toy gun for Bonnie; a suit, a flat cap, and a toy gun for Clyde. This is a classic, stylish duo costume.

The flat cap and the beret are the two most important elements.

### 4. Sherlock Holmes and Watson

Sherlock in a deerstalker hat, a pipe, and a magnifying glass; Watson in a bowler hat and a military uniform. This is a classic literary duo costume.

The deerstalker hat is the most important element for Sherlock.

### 5. Batman and Robin

Batman in a black costume with a bat symbol; Robin in a red and green costume with an "R" symbol. This is a classic superhero duo costume that suits two friends with a clear dynamic.

The masks are the most important elements for both costumes.

## Pop Culture Duos

### 6. Eleven and Mike (Stranger Things)

Eleven in a pink dress with a blonde wig and a bloody nose; Mike in a 1980s outfit with a walkie-talkie. This is a recognizable pop culture duo costume.

The bloody nose makeup is the most important element for Eleven.

### 7. Mia and Vincent (Pulp Fiction)

Mia in a white shirt, black trousers, and a black bob wig; Vincent in a black suit with a ponytail. This is a stylish, recognizable pop culture duo costume.

The black bob wig is the most important element for Mia.

### 8. Shrek and Donkey

Shrek in a green bodysuit or green face paint with brown vest and boots; Donkey in a grey outfit with ears and a tail. This is a funny, recognizable duo costume.

The green face paint is the most important element for Shrek.

### 9. Buzz Lightyear and Woody

Buzz in a white and green space ranger costume; Woody in a cowboy hat, plaid shirt, and jeans with a pull-string on the back. This is a classic animated duo costume.

The cowboy hat is the most important element for Woody.

### 10. Moana and Maui

Moana in a teal and red outfit with a flower in her hair; Maui in a brown outfit with a fishhook prop and tattoo body paint. This is a colorful, recognizable duo costume.

The fishhook prop is the most important element for Maui.

## Punny Duos

### 11. Salt and Pepper

One person in a white outfit labeled "Salt," the other in a black outfit labeled "Pepper." This is the most classic punny duo costume and always gets a reaction.

Use large, readable labels — the pun needs to be visible from across the room.

### 12. Peanut Butter and Jelly

One person in a brown outfit labeled "Peanut Butter," the other in a purple outfit labeled "Jelly." This is a cheerful, recognizable punny duo costume.

Use large, readable labels — the pun needs to be visible from across the room.

### 13. Devil and Angel

One person in a red outfit with horns and a tail; the other in a white outfit with wings and a halo. This is the most classic duo costume and suits two friends with contrasting personalities.

The horns and the halo are the two most important elements.

### 14. Ketchup and Mustard

One person in a red outfit labeled "Ketchup," the other in a yellow outfit labeled "Mustard." This is a simple, recognizable punny duo costume.

Use large, readable labels — the pun needs to be visible from across the room.

### 15. Plug and Socket

One person dressed as an electrical plug, the other as a socket. This is a clever, slightly cheeky punny duo costume.

The props are the most important elements — make them large and readable.

## Themed Duos

### 16. Witch and Black Cat

One person in a witch costume; the other in a black cat costume. This is a classic Halloween duo that suits two friends who want to look good rather than funny.

The witch hat and the cat ears are the two most important elements.

### 17. Vampire and Victim

One person in a vampire costume; the other in a white outfit with fake bite marks on the neck. This is a classic Halloween duo with a clear narrative.

The bite mark makeup is the most important element for the victim.

### 18. Frankenstein and His Bride

Frankenstein in a dark suit with green face paint and bolts; the Bride in a white dress with a tall, streaked wig. This is a classic Halloween duo that suits two friends with a sense of drama.

The tall, streaked wig is the most important element for the Bride.

### 19. Pirate and Mermaid

One person in a pirate costume; the other in a mermaid tail and a shell top. This is a colorful, recognizable duo costume that suits two friends with contrasting styles.

The mermaid tail is the most important element — choose a comfortable one you can walk in.

### 20. Astronaut and Alien

One person in a white astronaut suit; the other in a green outfit with alien antennae. This is a fun, recognizable duo costume that suits two friends with a sense of humor.

The antennae are the most important element for the alien.

## Final Thoughts

The best best friend Halloween costumes are the ones that reflect the actual friendship. Choose a pairing that suits both personalities, coordinate the execution level, and commit to the characters for the whole evening. A duo costume that tells a story is always more memorable than two unrelated costumes worn by people who happen to be standing together.
