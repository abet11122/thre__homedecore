Wedding aisle decor frames the most important walk of the ceremony. It should draw the eye towards the altar, complement the overall wedding aesthetic, and create a beautiful backdrop for photographs — without competing with the couple or the ceremony backdrop.

The ideas below cover every aisle decor style, from simple ribbon to elaborate floral installations.

## 1. Pew End Flowers

Flowers or greenery tied to the end of each pew or chair create a classic, elegant aisle. Use a single flower variety — roses, peonies, or hydrangeas — for a formal look, or a loose bunch of wildflowers and greenery for a more relaxed aesthetic.

Tie them with ribbon, twine, or a simple knot of greenery.

## 2. Lanterns Along the Aisle

Lanterns placed at intervals along the aisle, each with a pillar candle or LED insert, create a warm, romantic atmosphere. Use matching lanterns in the same size and finish for a cohesive look.

This works especially well for an evening ceremony or a ceremony in a dimly lit venue.

## 3. A Petal Runner

A path of rose petals, dried petals, or lavender laid along the aisle creates a romantic, photogenic detail. Use a consistent colour — all white, all blush, or a mix of the wedding colours.

Dried petals are more practical than fresh ones as they do not wilt or stain.

## 4. Ribbon on Chairs

A length of ribbon tied to the back of each chair or pew is one of the simplest and most elegant aisle decorations. Use ribbon in the wedding colours or a neutral cream or ivory.

This works for both indoor and outdoor ceremonies.

## 5. A Greenery Garland

A garland of eucalyptus, ferns, or ivy draped along the chairs or pews creates a lush, natural aisle. This is one of the most cost-effective aisle decorations as greenery is much less expensive than flowers.

## 6. Floral Hoops

Circular floral hoops — a wire ring covered with flowers and greenery — hung from the end of each pew or placed on stands along the aisle create a modern, romantic look.

## 7. Candles in Glass Holders

Pillar candles in glass hurricane holders placed at intervals along the aisle create a warm, intimate atmosphere. Use candles of the same height for a clean, formal look, or vary the heights for a more relaxed result.

## 8. A Floral Arch at the Aisle Entrance

A floral arch at the entrance to the aisle creates a dramatic moment as the couple walks through it. Use flowers and greenery that match the ceremony backdrop.

## 9. Potted Plants

Potted plants — topiaries, olive trees, or large ferns — placed at intervals along the aisle create a lush, garden-inspired look. They can be reused as reception decorations after the ceremony.

## 10. A Fabric Canopy

A fabric canopy — lengths of sheer fabric draped between poles along the aisle — creates a romantic, ethereal atmosphere. Use fabric in the wedding colours or a neutral white or ivory.

## Final Thoughts

Wedding aisle decor is most effective when it frames the couple and draws the eye to the altar without competing with it. Start with the pew ends and the aisle entrance, then add a petal runner and candles. The aisle decor should feel like a natural extension of the overall wedding aesthetic.
