A funny couple costume has one job: make people laugh. The best ones have a single, clear joke that lands the moment people see you together. The ideas below are chosen for their clarity and their commitment potential.

## Food Puns

### 1. Deviled Egg and Angel Egg

One person in a white outfit with a yellow circle on the front and devil horns; the other in a white outfit with a yellow circle and angel wings and a halo. Together you are a deviled egg and an angel egg.

The yellow circles need to be large enough to be readable from across the room.

### 2. Salt and Pepper

One person in a white outfit labeled "Salt," the other in a black outfit labeled "Pepper." This is the most classic punny couple costume and always gets a reaction.

Use large, readable labels — the pun needs to be visible from across the room.

### 3. Ketchup and Mustard

One person in a red outfit labeled "Ketchup," the other in a yellow outfit labeled "Mustard." This is a simple, recognizable punny couple costume.

Use large, readable labels — the pun needs to be visible from across the room.

### 4. Peanut Butter and Jelly

One person in a brown outfit labeled "Peanut Butter," the other in a purple outfit labeled "Jelly." This is a cheerful, recognizable punny couple costume.

Use large, readable labels — the pun needs to be visible from across the room.

### 5. Fork and Spoon

One person in a silver outfit shaped like a fork (with a fork prop or a fork-shaped sign); the other in a silver outfit shaped like a spoon. Together you are a "fork and spoon" — or, if you are feeling cheeky, you are "spooning."

The props are the most important elements — make them large and clearly recognizable.

### 6. Avocado and Toast

One person in a green avocado costume; the other in a beige outfit with brown squares as a piece of toast. Together you are avocado toast — the most millennial couple costume possible.

The avocado pit is the most important element for the avocado costume.

## Tech and Pop Culture Puns

### 7. Plug and Socket

One person dressed as an electrical plug, the other as a socket. This is a clever, slightly cheeky punny couple costume that always gets a knowing laugh.

The props are the most important elements — make them large and readable.

### 8. Netflix and Chill

One person in a Netflix-branded outfit; the other in a blanket and pajamas with a remote control. This is a funny, relatable couple costume.

The Netflix logo needs to be large and clearly readable.

### 9. WiFi and Dead Zone

One person in an outfit covered in WiFi signal bars; the other in an outfit with a "No Signal" symbol. This is a clever, topical pun that suits a tech-savvy couple.

The signal bars and the no-signal symbol need to be large and clearly readable.

### 10. Siri and Alexa

One person in an Apple-branded outfit labeled "Siri"; the other in an Amazon-branded outfit labeled "Alexa." Respond to every question in a robotic voice and give slightly unhelpful answers.

The robotic voices and the unhelpful answers are what make this costume funny.

### 11. Error 404 and Found

One person in a plain outfit with a sign that says "Error 404: Not Found"; the other in a bright outfit with a sign that says "Found!" This is a clever, tech-savvy pun that suits a couple with a sense of humor.

The contrast between the two signs is the joke.

## Relationship Puns

### 12. Ceiling Fan and Floor Fan

One person in a plain outfit with a sign that says "Go Ceilings!"; the other in a plain outfit with a sign that says "Go Floors!" This is a punny, low-effort couple costume that always gets a laugh.

The signs need to be large and readable — the pun only works if people can read them.

### 13. Formal Apology and Formal Complaint

One person in a formal outfit with a sign that says "Formal Apology"; the other in a formal outfit with a sign that says "Formal Complaint." This is a funny, slightly passive-aggressive couple costume.

The formal outfits are the most important element — the contrast between the formality and the message is the joke.

### 14. Swipe Right and Swipe Left

One person in an attractive outfit with a green "Swipe Right" sign; the other in a less attractive outfit with a red "Swipe Left" sign. This is a funny, dating-app-era couple costume.

The contrast between the two signs is the joke.

### 15. Introvert and Extrovert

One person in a quiet, understated outfit with a sign that says "Introvert"; the other in a loud, colorful outfit with a sign that says "Extrovert." This is a funny, relatable couple costume that suits a couple with contrasting personalities.

The contrast between the two outfits is the joke.

## Classic Funny Pairings

### 16. Shrek and Fiona

Shrek in a green bodysuit or green face paint with brown vest and boots; Fiona in a green dress with a red braid. This is a funny, recognizable couple costume that suits a couple with a sense of humor about their relationship.

The green face paint is the most important element for both characters.

### 17. Ken and Barbie (Reversed)

The woman in a "Ken" outfit — pink polo shirt, khakis, and a "Ken" name tag; the man in a "Barbie" outfit — a pink dress and a blonde wig. This is a funny, gender-swapped couple costume.

The gender swap is the joke — commit to it completely.

### 18. A Selfie

One person holds a large printed frame in front of them as if taking a selfie; the other stands behind the frame as the subject of the selfie. Add a printed phone prop for extra effect.

The frame needs to be large enough to frame the subject's face clearly.

## Final Thoughts

The best funny couple costumes are the ones where both partners are equally committed to the joke. Choose a concept you both find genuinely funny, make the pun readable from across the room, and commit to the characters for the whole evening. A couple that is clearly having fun with their costume is always funnier than a couple that is wearing a funny costume reluctantly.
