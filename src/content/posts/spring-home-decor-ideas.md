Spring home decor is most effective when it is approached as a whole-home update rather than a room-by-room project. A consistent palette and a few key changes in each room create a cohesive seasonal atmosphere that feels genuinely fresh.

The ideas below are organised by room, from the entryway to the bedroom.

## Entryway

<!-- IDEA 1: [MY MEASUREMENTS / COST / TIME: record constraints for this option]. [WHAT I TRIED AND WHAT WENT WRONG: actual result only]. Add suitability, tradeoff, installation and maintenance evidence. -->

The entryway sets the tone for the whole house. A spring wreath on the door, a new doormat in a spring colour, and a vase of fresh flowers on the console table create a welcoming spring entrance.

Swap the heavy winter coat hooks for lighter spring jackets and add a potted plant or a small bunch of herbs on the windowsill.

## Living Room

<!-- IDEA 2: [MY MEASUREMENTS / COST / TIME: record constraints for this option]. [WHAT I TRIED AND WHAT WENT WRONG: actual result only]. Add suitability, tradeoff, installation and maintenance evidence. -->

The living room benefits most from a textile swap. Replace heavy cushion covers with lighter ones in sage green, blush, or warm cream. Swap the wool throw for a linen one.

Add a generous vase of budding branches or fresh flowers as the main seasonal focal point. Style the coffee table with a spring vignette — a small plant, a light candle, and a botanical print.

## Kitchen

<!-- IDEA 3: [MY MEASUREMENTS / COST / TIME: record constraints for this option]. [WHAT I TRIED AND WHAT WENT WRONG: actual result only]. Add suitability, tradeoff, installation and maintenance evidence. -->

A bunch of fresh herbs in a small pot on the windowsill — basil, mint, or rosemary — adds fragrance and a living green element to the kitchen.

Replace a dark winter fruit bowl with a lighter ceramic one and fill it with lemons, limes, or spring fruit. Add a floral tea towel for a small seasonal touch.

## Dining Room

<!-- IDEA 4: [MY MEASUREMENTS / COST / TIME: record constraints for this option]. [WHAT I TRIED AND WHAT WENT WRONG: actual result only]. Add suitability, tradeoff, installation and maintenance evidence. -->

A spring table runner in a floral print or natural linen, a vase of fresh flowers as the centerpiece, and cloth napkins in a spring colour create a complete spring dining room update.

Add a botanical print to the wall or lean a framed pressed flower arrangement on the sideboard.

## Bedroom

<!-- IDEA 5: [MY MEASUREMENTS / COST / TIME: record constraints for this option]. [WHAT I TRIED AND WHAT WENT WRONG: actual result only]. Add suitability, tradeoff, installation and maintenance evidence. -->

Swap heavy winter bedding for a lighter duvet or a cotton quilt. Replace dark cushion covers with ones in soft blush, sage, or pale yellow.

Add a small vase of flowers on the bedside table and a potted plant on the windowsill. Replace a heavy winter candle with a lighter spring fragrance.

## Bathroom

<!-- IDEA 6: [MY MEASUREMENTS / COST / TIME: record constraints for this option]. [WHAT I TRIED AND WHAT WENT WRONG: actual result only]. Add suitability, tradeoff, installation and maintenance evidence. -->

A small potted plant — a fern, a succulent, or a trailing ivy — adds a living element to the bathroom. Replace dark winter towels with lighter ones in a spring colour.

Add a spring-scented hand soap and a small vase of fresh flowers or eucalyptus on the shelf.

## Home Office

<!-- IDEA 7: [MY MEASUREMENTS / COST / TIME: record constraints for this option]. [WHAT I TRIED AND WHAT WENT WRONG: actual result only]. Add suitability, tradeoff, installation and maintenance evidence. -->

A potted plant on the desk, a botanical print on the wall, and a lighter desk accessory in a spring colour are enough to make a home office feel seasonal.

Replace a heavy winter mug with a lighter ceramic one and add a small vase of flowers beside the monitor.

## Maximising Natural Light

<!-- IDEA 8: [MY MEASUREMENTS / COST / TIME: record constraints for this option]. [WHAT I TRIED AND WHAT WENT WRONG: actual result only]. Add suitability, tradeoff, installation and maintenance evidence. -->

The most powerful spring home decor change costs nothing. Clean the windows, swap heavy curtains for lighter sheers or linen panels, and move furniture away from windows to let in as much natural light as possible.

Natural light transforms a room more than any decoration.

## A Consistent Spring Palette

<!-- IDEA 9: [MY MEASUREMENTS / COST / TIME: record constraints for this option]. [WHAT I TRIED AND WHAT WENT WRONG: actual result only]. Add suitability, tradeoff, installation and maintenance evidence. -->

Choose two or three colours and use them consistently across every room — the same sage green cushion in the living room and the bedroom, the same blush in the bathroom and the entryway. This creates a cohesive spring atmosphere that feels intentional rather than assembled from separate seasonal purchases.

## Final Thoughts

<!-- IDEA 10: [MY MEASUREMENTS / COST / TIME: record constraints for this option]. [WHAT I TRIED AND WHAT WENT WRONG: actual result only]. Add suitability, tradeoff, installation and maintenance evidence. -->

Spring home decor is about lightness, freshness, and natural beauty. Start with the textiles and the light, add fresh flowers and plants, and use a consistent palette across the whole home. The result should feel like the season itself — bright, welcoming, and genuinely alive.

### Keep the Post-Holiday Greenery Simple

<!-- IDEA 12: [MY MEASUREMENTS / COST / TIME: record constraints for this option]. [WHAT I TRIED AND WHAT WENT WRONG: actual result only]. Add suitability, tradeoff, installation and maintenance evidence. -->

After Christmas, swap themed holiday greenery for simple evergreen branches, dried eucalyptus, or a single stem arrangement. This extends the seasonal feeling without looking festive.

Start with what you already own, then make one small change at a time.

### Add More Texture, Not More Objects

<!-- IDEA 13: [MY MEASUREMENTS / COST / TIME: record constraints for this option]. [WHAT I TRIED AND WHAT WENT WRONG: actual result only]. Add suitability, tradeoff, installation and maintenance evidence. -->

Wool throws, boucle cushions, knit blankets, and velvet cushion covers add warmth and depth without cluttering surfaces. This is especially useful in small rooms where too many objects can feel busy.

Choose washable or easy-care materials in high-use areas.

### Swap the Textiles First

<!-- IDEA 14: [MY MEASUREMENTS / COST / TIME: record constraints for this option]. [WHAT I TRIED AND WHAT WENT WRONG: actual result only]. Add suitability, tradeoff, installation and maintenance evidence. -->

The fastest spring decor change is swapping heavy winter cushion covers and throws for lighter ones. Replace wool and velvet with linen, cotton, and light knits in spring colours — soft blush, sage green, pale yellow, or warm cream.

This single change shifts the whole mood of a room before you add a single decoration.

### Bring in Fresh Flowers

<!-- IDEA 15: [MY MEASUREMENTS / COST / TIME: record constraints for this option]. [WHAT I TRIED AND WHAT WENT WRONG: actual result only]. Add suitability, tradeoff, installation and maintenance evidence. -->

A vase of fresh tulips, daffodils, or hyacinths is the most powerful spring decoration available. Place them where they will be seen first — the kitchen counter, the dining table, or the entryway console.

Change the water every two days and cut the stems at an angle to extend their life.
