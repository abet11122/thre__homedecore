A backyard wedding is one of the most personal and beautiful wedding settings available. The familiar space becomes something extraordinary with the right decorations, and the garden's existing features — trees, hedges, and paths — do much of the decorating work for free.

The ideas below cover every element of backyard wedding decor, from the ceremony to the reception.

## 1. String Lights Overhead

String lights hung overhead — between trees, along a fence, or from a temporary structure — are the single most transformative backyard wedding decoration. They create a magical atmosphere after dark and look beautiful in photographs.

Use warm-white lights around 2700K for the most romantic effect.

## 2. Define the Ceremony Space

Create a clear ceremony space with a defined aisle and a focal point — an arch, a backdrop, or a natural feature of the garden.

Use potted plants, lanterns, or ribbon to define the aisle. Position the ceremony to take advantage of the best view in the garden.

## 3. The Ceremony Arch

A ceremony arch covered with flowers and greenery creates the focal point of the backyard wedding ceremony. Use a wooden arch, a metal arch, or a natural branch arch.

Position it against the most beautiful part of the garden — a hedge, a tree, or a garden wall.

## 4. The Reception Tables

Set the reception tables with centerpieces, runners, and candles that suit the garden setting. Use natural materials — wood, linen, and greenery — for the most cohesive outdoor look.

See the [wedding reception table decor ideas](/post/wedding-reception-table-decor-ideas/) post for a full guide.

## 5. A Marquee or Tent

A marquee or tent provides shelter and defines the reception space. Choose a clear-sided marquee to maintain the garden view, or a fabric tent for a more romantic atmosphere.

Rent rather than buy — marquees are expensive to purchase and difficult to store.

## 6. Potted Plants as Decorations

Large potted plants — olive trees, topiaries, or tall ferns — placed throughout the garden create a lush, garden-party atmosphere and can be kept after the wedding.

## 7. A Flower Wall or Backdrop

A flower wall or a fabric backdrop creates a beautiful photo opportunity and a focal point for the reception space.

## 8. Lanterns Along the Path

Lanterns placed along the garden path guide guests from the ceremony to the reception and add warmth after dark.

## 9. A Drinks Station

A styled drinks station — a bar cart, a wooden table, or a vintage sideboard — in the garden creates a focal point for the pre-reception drinks and adds a personal touch.

## 10. Personal Touches

Photos of the couple, a guest book, personalised signage, and favours that reflect the couple's personality make a backyard wedding feel genuinely personal rather than like a hired venue.

## Practical Considerations

- **Power:** ensure there is enough power for string lights, catering equipment, and music
- **Toilets:** hire portable toilets if the house cannot accommodate all guests
- **Parking:** arrange parking for all guests
- **Neighbours:** inform neighbours in advance and check local noise regulations

## Final Thoughts

A backyard wedding is most beautiful when it works with the garden rather than trying to transform it into something it is not. Start with string lights and a ceremony arch, define the spaces clearly, and add personal touches that reflect the couple. The garden itself is the most beautiful decoration available.
