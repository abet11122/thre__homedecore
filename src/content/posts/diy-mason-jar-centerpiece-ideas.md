Mason jar centerpieces are one of the most versatile and cost-effective decorating formats available. They suit weddings, baby showers, bridal showers, garden parties, and everyday home styling — and they can be assembled in under 30 minutes with flowers from a supermarket or garden.

The ideas below cover every occasion and every style.

## The Basic Method

1. Fill mason jars with fresh water — about two-thirds full
2. Cut flower stems at an angle, removing any leaves that will sit below the waterline
3. Arrange flowers and greenery in the jar, starting with the greenery and adding flowers on top
4. Group three to five jars together on a wooden board or tray
5. Tie twine, ribbon, or burlap around the neck of each jar
6. Add a candle in one of the jars for warmth

## 1. The Wildflower Collection

Fill a grouping of mason jars with loose bunches of wildflowers — cornflowers, cosmos, poppies, and grasses — in a mix of heights and colours.

This is the most relaxed and informal mason jar centerpiece style, and one of the most charming.

## 2. The Single-Colour Arrangement

Fill all the jars in a grouping with flowers in a single colour — all white, all blush, or all yellow — for a more sophisticated, cohesive look.

Use different flower varieties in the same colour for the most interesting result.

## 3. The Candle and Flower Mix

Fill some jars with flowers and others with candles — a pillar candle or a tea light — for a mix of light and colour in the grouping.

This reduces the flower count and adds warmth that a purely floral arrangement cannot achieve.

## 4. The Herb and Flower Arrangement

Fill mason jars with a mix of fresh herbs — rosemary, lavender, and mint — and simple flowers. The herbs add fragrance and a natural, garden quality.

This works especially well for a garden party, a bridal shower, or a rustic wedding.

## 5. The Seasonal Arrangement

Adapt the mason jar centerpiece to the season:
- **Spring:** tulips, daffodils, and eucalyptus
- **Summer:** sunflowers, zinnias, and lavender
- **Autumn:** dahlias, dried grasses, and berries
- **Winter:** pine, holly, and white roses

## 6. The Fairy Light Jar

Fill a mason jar with a string of battery-powered fairy lights for a warm, glowing centerpiece that requires no flowers.

Group three to five fairy light jars together on a wooden board for the most effective display.

## 7. The Painted Jar

Paint mason jars in a colour that suits the occasion — blush for a bridal shower, sage green for a garden party, or deep burgundy for a winter wedding.

Use chalk paint for a matte, vintage look or spray paint for a more even finish.

## 8. The Frosted Jar

Apply frosted glass spray to mason jars for a soft, diffused look. Fill with a candle or a simple flower arrangement.

Frosted jars look particularly beautiful with a candle inside, as the frosting diffuses the light.

## 9. The Wrapped Jar

Wrap mason jars in burlap, lace, or twine for a rustic, textural look. Secure with a hot glue gun and tie a ribbon or bow at the top.

## 10. The Tiered Display

Place mason jars at different heights using wooden blocks, books, or a tiered stand. This creates a more dynamic display than a flat grouping.

## Final Thoughts
