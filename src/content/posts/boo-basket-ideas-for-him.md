A boo basket for him works best when it is built around things he will actually use rather than things that look good in a basket. The ideas below are organized by category so you can build a basket around his interests and preferences.

## Snacks and Drinks

### 1. His Favorite Snacks

The simplest and most appreciated boo basket item is a collection of his favorite snacks — chips, jerky, nuts, popcorn, or whatever he reaches for first. Buy the good versions rather than the generic ones.

A mix of sweet and savory is more interesting than all of one type.

### 2. Specialty Beer or Cider

A four-pack of a craft beer or a seasonal cider he would not normally buy himself is a generous boo basket item. Choose a fall flavor — pumpkin ale, apple cider, or a dark stout.

Check that he drinks alcohol before including it.

### 3. Whiskey or Bourbon Miniatures

A set of whiskey or bourbon miniatures in different styles — smoky, sweet, spicy — is a fun way to let him try new things without committing to a full bottle.

Choose miniatures from distilleries he has not tried before.

### 4. Specialty Coffee or Hot Chocolate

A bag of specialty coffee in a fall roast — dark, smoky, or with notes of chocolate and caramel — or a premium hot chocolate mix is a practical gift he will use every morning.

Choose whole beans if he has a grinder, ground if he does not.

### 5. Halloween Candy Mix

A bag of mixed Halloween candy — candy corn, pumpkin-shaped chocolates, caramel apples — fills the basket and adds a festive touch. Choose a mix that includes his favorites.

Avoid candy he is allergic to — check before buying.

### 6. Spiced Nuts or Beef Jerky

A bag of spiced nuts or premium beef jerky is a savory, practical snack that suits a boo basket for him. Choose a flavor he already likes — spicy, smoky, or sweet and salty.

Choose a resealable bag so he can save some for later.

### 7. Hot Sauce Set

A set of hot sauces in different heat levels is a fun, practical gift for someone who likes spicy food. Choose a set with at least three different sauces.

Include a card that rates each sauce by heat level.

## Grooming and Self-Care

### 8. Quality Shaving Kit

A quality shaving cream, a new razor, and an aftershave balm in a fall scent — sandalwood, cedar, or leather — is a practical self-care gift he will use every day.

Choose a scent he already wears if you know it.

### 9. Lip Balm

A simple, unflavored or lightly scented lip balm is a practical gift that most men appreciate but rarely buy for themselves. Choose a formula with SPF for extra points.

A two-pack means he can keep one at home and one in his bag.

### 10. Hand Cream

A non-greasy hand cream in a neutral or woody scent is a practical gift for someone who works with their hands. Choose a tube rather than a jar for easier application.

A hand cream with SPF is a thoughtful upgrade.

## Entertainment and Hobbies

### 11. Halloween Movie Night Kit

A bag of popcorn, a bag of candy, and a list of recommended Halloween movies printed on a card creates a complete movie night kit. Add a cozy pair of socks for extra points.

Choose movies at the right scare level — not everyone wants to watch horror.

### 12. Card Game or Puzzle

A Halloween-themed card game, a small puzzle, or a trivia game is a fun gift for someone who enjoys games. Choose something that can be played solo or with a partner.

A card game is more portable and easier to store than a board game.

### 13. Book

A Halloween-themed novel, a thriller, or a book about a topic he is interested in is a thoughtful gift for a reader. Choose a book he has not read rather than a classic he already owns.

Add a bookmark with a Halloween print for extra detail.

### 14. Cozy Socks

A pair of thick, cozy socks in a Halloween print — black cats, pumpkins, ghosts — or in a fall color — charcoal, navy, or forest green — is a practical gift that always gets used.

Choose a size that fits most adults — most cozy socks are one-size-fits-most.

## Practical and Useful

### 15. Multi-Tool or Pocket Knife

A small multi-tool or a quality pocket knife is a practical gift for someone who likes to be prepared. Choose a compact model that fits in a pocket or a bag.

Check local laws regarding pocket knife blade length before buying.

### 16. Phone Stand or Charging Cable

A compact phone stand or a quality charging cable is a practical gift he will use every day. Choose a cable in a length that suits his setup — longer is usually more useful.

A braided cable lasts longer than a standard one.

### 17. Cozy Beanie or Hat

A chunky knit beanie in a fall color — charcoal, navy, or forest green — is a practical gift as the weather gets cooler. Choose a style that suits his everyday wardrobe.

A beanie with a fleece lining is warmer than a standard knit one.

### 18. Candle in a Masculine Scent

A candle in a woody, smoky, or leather scent — cedar, sandalwood, tobacco, or woodsmoke — is a gift he will actually burn. Choose a quality candle in a glass jar he can reuse.

Look for candles with a burn time of at least 40 hours for the best value.

## Final Thoughts

The best boo basket for him is the one that feels personal rather than generic. Start with his favorite snacks and one item related to something he enjoys, then fill the gaps with practical treats. A black or orange basket with tissue paper and a handwritten note makes even a modest collection of gifts feel special and intentional.
