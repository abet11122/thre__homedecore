Farmhouse Christmas decor is one of the most achievable holiday styles because it is built on materials that are inexpensive, widely available, and genuinely warm. It does not require a matching set of anything — in fact, the slight imperfection of mismatched textures is part of the appeal.

The ideas below cover every room in the house, from the front door to the kitchen.

## 1. Use Galvanised Metal Containers

Galvanised metal buckets, tubs, and trays are one of the most versatile farmhouse Christmas materials. Use them as planters for evergreens, as containers for pine cones and ornaments, or as bases for candle arrangements.

The slightly industrial look of galvanised metal works beautifully against greenery and natural wood.

## 2. Add Plaid and Buffalo Check Everywhere

Plaid and buffalo check in red, black, or green are the signature fabrics of farmhouse Christmas decor. Use them for throw pillows, blankets, table runners, ribbons, and stockings.

You do not need to match every plaid exactly — mixing different scales of the same pattern adds depth.

## 3. Fill Wooden Crates With Greenery

Wooden crates filled with fresh or faux greenery, pine cones, and a few ornaments make simple, farmhouse-style displays that work on the porch, in the living room, or on the kitchen counter.

Stack two or three crates at different heights for a more layered look.

## 4. Use Mason Jars as Vases and Candle Holders

Mason jars filled with greenery, berries, or a single candle are a farmhouse staple. Group three or five together on a tray for a simple, cohesive display.

Tie a length of twine or plaid ribbon around the neck of each jar for a finishing touch.

## 5. Hang a Simple Greenery Wreath

A plain greenery wreath with a plaid ribbon is more farmhouse than an elaborate decorated wreath. Choose pine, cedar, or eucalyptus for the most natural look.

Hang it on the front door, above the fireplace, or on a wall as a simple seasonal focal point.

## 6. Style the Mantelpiece With Natural Materials

A mantelpiece styled with fresh greenery, pillar candles in wooden or metal holders, pine cones, and a few simple ornaments is the heart of farmhouse Christmas decor.

Keep the arrangement asymmetrical and slightly imperfect — farmhouse style is not about precision.

## 7. Use a Wooden Advent Calendar

A wooden advent calendar — either a traditional numbered box style or a simple row of small bags hung on a branch — is a functional farmhouse decoration that children and adults both enjoy.

Fill it with small treats, notes, or activities rather than just sweets.

## 8. Add Dried Orange Slices and Cinnamon Sticks

Dried orange slices and cinnamon sticks are inexpensive, fragrant, and genuinely farmhouse in aesthetic. Use them in garlands, in bowl arrangements, or tied to gift wrapping.

Dry orange slices in the oven at a low temperature for two to three hours, or buy them pre-dried.

## 9. Use a Ladder as a Display

A wooden ladder leaned against a wall makes an unexpected and practical farmhouse display. Hang stockings, garlands, and ornaments from the rungs.

This works especially well in a small space where a full mantelpiece is not available.

## 10. Keep the Kitchen Farmhouse Too

A galvanised metal tray on the kitchen counter with a few candles, some greenery, and a small ornament extends the farmhouse Christmas look into the most-used room in the house.

A simple wreath on the kitchen window or a sprig of greenery in a mason jar on the windowsill is enough.

## Final Thoughts

Farmhouse Christmas decor is about warmth, texture, and honest materials rather than perfection. Start with greenery, plaid, and a few galvanised metal containers, and build from there. The style rewards a relaxed, imperfect approach — which makes it one of the most enjoyable holiday decorating styles to work with.
