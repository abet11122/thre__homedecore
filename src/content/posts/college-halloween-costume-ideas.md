College Halloween is competitive. Everyone is trying to be clever, and the bar for "good costume" is higher than it is at a family trick-or-treat. The ideas below are chosen for their specificity and their conversation-starting potential.

A costume built from thrift store finds and one specific accessory is almost always better than a cheap costume kit.

## Clever and Punny

### 1. Error 404: Costume Not Found

A plain outfit with a printed sign that says "Error 404: Costume Not Found." This is the laziest Halloween costume on the list, but it is also one of the funniest at a tech-savvy college party.

Use a large, readable font on the sign.

### 2. Ceiling Fan

A plain outfit with a sign that says "Go Ceilings!" Respond to every "What are you?" with enthusiastic ceiling cheering.

The commitment to the bit is what makes this costume funny.

### 3. Freudian Slip

A slip dress with the word "Freudian" written on it, or a slip dress with Freud's face printed on it. This is a clever, slightly intellectual pun that suits a college party.

The label needs to be large and readable — the pun only works if people can read it.

### 4. Identity Theft

A plain outfit covered in "Hello My Name Is" stickers with different names on each one. This is a clever, topical pun that suits a college party.

Use as many name tags as possible — the more, the funnier.

### 5. A Formal Apology

A formal suit or dress with a sign that says "I'm Sorry." This is a clever pun that works on two levels — the formal outfit and the apology.

Apologize to everyone you meet throughout the evening for extra effect.

### 6. A Participation Trophy

A plain outfit with a large "Participation Trophy" ribbon and a sign that says "You Tried." This is a funny, slightly self-deprecating costume that suits a college student with a good sense of humor.

The ribbon needs to be large and clearly readable.

### 7. A Nudist on Strike

Regular clothes with a sign that says "Nudist on Strike." This is the most minimal funny Halloween costume and one of the most reliably funny at a college party.

Use a large, readable font on the sign.

## Pop Culture

### 8. Walter White

A white hazmat suit or a yellow one, a bald cap or a shaved head, glasses, and a bag of blue rock candy labeled "Blue Sky." This is a recognizable pop culture costume that suits a college student with a sense of humor.

The bald cap and the glasses are the two most important elements.

### 9. The Dude (The Big Lebowski)

A bathrobe, a white t-shirt, jeans, sunglasses, and a White Russian drink. This is a comfortable, recognizable costume that suits a college student who wants to be comfortable.

The bathrobe is the most important element — choose a white or cream one.

### 10. Michael Scott

A suit with a "World's Best Boss" mug, a tie, and a slightly desperate expression. This is a recognizable pop culture costume that suits a college student who is a fan of The Office.

The "World's Best Boss" mug is the most important prop.

### 11. Dwight Schrute

A mustard yellow shirt, a brown tie, glasses, and a beet prop. This is a recognizable pop culture costume that suits a college student who is a fan of The Office.

The mustard yellow shirt and the glasses are the two most important elements.

### 12. Wednesday Addams

A black dress with a white collar, two braids, and a deadpan expression. This is a perennially popular college Halloween costume that suits a student with dark hair.

The deadpan expression is the most important element — Wednesday never smiles.

### 13. Eleven (Stranger Things)

A pink dress, a blonde wig, a bloody nose, and a box of Eggo waffles. This is a recognizable pop culture costume that suits a college student who is a fan of Stranger Things.

The bloody nose makeup and the Eggo waffles are the two most important elements.

### 14. Ken (from Barbie)

A pink outfit — pink polo shirt, pink shorts or trousers — with a "Ken" name tag. This is a topical, funny costume that suits a college student with a sense of humor.

The more pink, the better.

## Thrift Store Finds

### 15. A Specific Decade

Choose a specific decade — the 1970s, the 1980s, the 1990s — and dress in the most exaggerated version of that decade's fashion. Thrift stores are full of decade-appropriate clothing.

The more exaggerated, the better — subtlety does not work for decade costumes.

### 16. A Specific Profession

Choose a specific profession — a 1950s housewife, a 1980s stockbroker, a 1990s aerobics instructor — and dress in the most exaggerated version of that profession's uniform. Thrift stores are the best source for profession-specific clothing.

The more specific the profession, the funnier the costume.

### 17. A Thrift Store Painting

Find a thrift store painting of a person and dress as the person in the painting — matching the outfit, the hairstyle, and the expression as closely as possible. Carry the painting with you for comparison.

The closer the match between the costume and the painting, the funnier the costume.

### 18. A Specific Movie Character from a Specific Scene

Choose a very specific scene from a movie — not just "Indiana Jones" but "Indiana Jones in the opening scene of Raiders of the Lost Ark" — and dress as the character in that specific scene. The specificity is what makes it funny.

The more specific the scene, the more impressive the costume.

## Group Options for College

### 19. The Office Cast

Assign each person a specific Office character — Michael, Dwight, Jim, Pam, Kevin, Angela, Ryan. The group is recognizable as a set because of the specific character details.

The "World's Best Boss" mug is the most important prop for Michael.

### 20. Vine References

Each person dresses as a specific Vine reference — "It is Wednesday, my dudes," "Road work ahead," "I can't believe you've done this." This is a funny, nostalgic group costume for a college crowd.

The more obscure the Vine reference, the more impressive the costume.

### 21. A Group Chat

Each person wears a name tag with their group chat nickname and carries a printed phone screen showing a funny message exchange. This is a personal, inside-joke costume that suits a close friend group.

The funnier the message exchange, the better the costume.

### 22. Different Versions of the Same Person

Each person dresses as a different version of the same person — Monday morning version, Friday night version, exam week version, summer break version. This is a funny, relatable group costume for a college crowd.

The contrast between the different versions is the joke.

### 23. A Zoom Class

Each person wears a business outfit on top and pajamas on the bottom, with a name tag showing their Zoom display name. This is a relatable, post-pandemic group costume for a college crowd.

The contrast between the top and bottom halves is the joke.

### 24. The Stages of a College Night Out

Each person represents a different stage of a college night out — "Pre-drinks," "First bar," "Second bar," "3am kebab," "Next morning." This is a funny, relatable group costume for a college crowd.

The more specific and accurate the stages, the funnier the costume.

## Final Thoughts

The best college Halloween costumes are the ones that are specific, committed, and executed with confidence. Start with what you already own, add one specific accessory, and commit to the character for the whole evening. A simple costume worn with genuine enthusiasm is always more memorable than an elaborate one worn half-heartedly.
