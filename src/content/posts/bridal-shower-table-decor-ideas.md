A bridal shower table is the visual centrepiece of the celebration. Whether it is the food table, the dining table, or both, a beautifully set table creates the atmosphere that guests will remember and photograph.

The ideas below cover every element of bridal shower table decor.

## 1. The Table Runner

A table runner in the shower's colour palette — blush linen, white lace, or sage green cotton — sets the tone for the whole table.

Use the same runner on the food table and the dining table for a cohesive look.

## 2. The Centerpiece

A fresh flower arrangement, a bud vase collection, or a candle and flower display creates the visual focal point of the table.

See the [bridal shower centerpiece ideas](/post/bridal-shower-centerpiece-ideas/) post for a full guide.

## 3. The Place Setting

A plate on a charger or placemat, a folded napkin, and a small personal touch at each place creates a layered, considered place setting.

Use the same place setting across all tables for a cohesive look.

## 4. The Name Card

A name card at each place setting makes guests feel individually welcomed. Use a consistent design — the same font, the same card stock, and the same style.

Add a dried flower or a sprig of greenery under each card for a finishing touch.

## 5. The Favour

A small favour at each place setting — a personalised candle, a small jar of honey, or a homemade treat — doubles as a decoration and a thank-you gift.

## 6. The Napkin

A cloth napkin in the shower's colour palette, folded simply and tied with a ribbon or a sprig of rosemary, adds a personal touch to each place setting.

## 7. The Food Table

The food table should be styled with tiered stands, fresh flowers, matching serving dishes, and labels for each food item.

Add a sign with the bride's name or a bridal shower message above the table.

## 8. The Drinks Station

A styled drinks station — a bar cart, a wooden table, or a sideboard — with matching glasses, a pitcher of punch or prosecco, and a few flowers creates a welcoming focal point.

## 9. Candles

Candles on the table add warmth and romance. Use them on the dining table and the food table, in stable holders and away from overhanging fabric.

## 10. Fresh Flowers Throughout

Fresh flowers on every surface — the dining table, the food table, the drinks station, and the entrance — create a generous, celebratory atmosphere.

Use flowers in the shower's colour palette and choose varieties that last well at room temperature.

## Final Thoughts

A beautiful bridal shower table comes from a consistent colour palette, fresh flowers, and a few personal touches at each place. Start with the runner and the centerpiece, set the places, and add candles and flowers throughout. The result should feel genuinely celebratory and genuinely welcoming.
