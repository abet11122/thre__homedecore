The front door is the most visible part of your home's exterior. A well-decorated fall front door makes a strong first impression from the street and sets the tone for the whole house.

The ideas below cover every element of a fall front door display, from the wreath to the doormat.

## 1. Choose the Right Wreath

A wreath is the foundation of any fall front door display. Choose one that is roughly half the width of the door for the best proportion.

Dried botanical wreaths — pampas grass, eucalyptus, dried sunflowers — look more sophisticated than artificial leaf rings and last the whole season.

## 2. Add a Garland Around the Frame

A garland draped around the door frame adds depth and generosity that a wreath alone cannot achieve. Use fresh or dried eucalyptus, pine, or autumn foliage.

Secure it at the corners and let it bow gently across the top.

## 3. Consider the Door Colour

The door colour is the backdrop for all your fall decorations. Deep green, navy, black, and burgundy all provide a strong backdrop for autumn decorations.

If your door is a neutral colour, choose decorations with stronger autumn tones. If your door is already a strong colour, choose more neutral decorations.

## 4. Add Lanterns on Either Side

Two matching lanterns on either side of the door, each with a pillar candle or LED insert, add warmth and a finished look. Choose lanterns in black, bronze, or galvanised metal.

Lanterns are a year-round investment that works for every season.

## 5. Use a Seasonal Doormat

A doormat with a fall message or pattern is an inexpensive update that changes the feel of the entrance immediately.

Choose a mat that fits the step without overhanging the edges and has a rubber backing to prevent slipping.

## 6. Add a Seasonal Sign

A wooden sign with a fall message — "Welcome", "Hello Autumn", or "Harvest" — hung beside the door or incorporated into the wreath adds a personal touch.

## 7. Use Potted Plants to Frame the Door

Two matching potted plants — mums, ornamental kale, or small evergreens — on either side of the door create a symmetrical, welcoming entrance.

Plant them in containers that suit the door style — galvanised metal for a farmhouse look, terracotta for a traditional look, or sleek black for a modern look.

## 8. Add a Pumpkin Grouping on the Step

A grouping of three to five pumpkins and gourds on the step beside the door adds a seasonal note at ground level.

Keep the colour palette consistent — all white, all orange, or a mix of cream and rust.

## 9. Use Corn Stalks for Height

Corn stalks tied to the door frame or porch post add height and a genuinely agricultural quality to the fall front door display.

Tie them with a length of twine or a plaid ribbon.

## 10. Light the Door for the Evenings

As the evenings get darker, lighting becomes more important. A lantern with a candle, solar stake lights along the path, or a string of warm-white lights around the door frame all add warmth after dark.

## Final Thoughts

A beautiful fall front door display starts with the right wreath and builds outward from there. Choose a wreath that suits your door colour and style, add a garland if the frame allows it, and complete the display with lanterns, a doormat, and a pumpkin grouping. The result should look welcoming from the street and beautiful up close.
