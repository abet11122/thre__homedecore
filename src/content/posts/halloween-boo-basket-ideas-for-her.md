A Halloween boo basket is a small gift left anonymously on someone's doorstep — the Halloween equivalent of a May Day basket. The tradition is to leave it, ring the bell, and run before you are seen.

The best boo baskets are personal rather than generic. The ideas below are organized by category so you can build a basket around what she actually likes.

## Cozy Gifts

### 1. Fall Candle

A candle in a fall scent — pumpkin spice, apple cider, woodsmoke, or cinnamon — is the most universally appreciated boo basket item. Choose a quality candle in a glass jar she can reuse.

Look for candles with a burn time of at least 40 hours for the best value.

### 2. Cozy Socks

A pair of thick, cozy socks in a Halloween print — black cats, pumpkins, ghosts — or in a fall color — rust, mustard, forest green — is a practical gift that always gets used.

Choose a size that fits most adults — most cozy socks are one-size-fits-most.

### 3. Chunky Knit Mug

A chunky ceramic mug in a fall color — terracotta, sage, or cream — is a gift she will use every morning through the season. Fill it with a few hot chocolate sachets or tea bags.

Choose a mug with a handle large enough for a comfortable grip.

### 4. Cozy Blanket or Throw

A small throw blanket in a fall color or a Halloween print is a generous boo basket item that she will use all season. Choose a soft material — fleece, faux fur, or chunky knit.

Fold it neatly and place it at the bottom of the basket as the base layer.

### 5. Halloween Pajama Set

A Halloween-print pajama set — pumpkins, ghosts, or black cats — is a fun, cozy gift that suits the season. Choose a soft material and a relaxed fit.

Check the size before buying — pajamas are not one-size-fits-most.

## Beauty and Self-Care

### 6. Face Mask

A sheet mask or a clay mask in a Halloween-themed packaging — or simply a high-quality mask she would not buy herself — is a practical self-care gift. Add a pair of under-eye patches for extra points.

Choose a mask suited to her skin type if you know it.

### 7. Hand Cream

A rich hand cream in a fall scent — pumpkin, vanilla, or sandalwood — is a practical gift that gets used every day. Choose a tube rather than a jar for easier application.

A hand cream with SPF is a thoughtful upgrade.

### 8. Bath Bomb or Bath Salts

A Halloween-themed bath bomb or a jar of fall-scented bath salts is a luxurious self-care gift. Choose a scent she already likes rather than something unfamiliar.

Wrap the bath bomb in tissue paper so it does not crumble in the basket.

### 9. Nail Polish

A nail polish in a Halloween color — deep burgundy, matte black, dark plum, or burnt orange — is a small, personal gift. Choose a brand she already uses if you know it.

Add a nail file and a cuticle oil for a complete nail care set.

### 10. Lip Balm Set

A set of lip balms in fall flavors — pumpkin spice, apple cider, cinnamon — is a small, practical gift that fills the basket without taking up much space.

Choose a set with at least three flavors for variety.

## Snacks and Treats

### 11. Her Favorite Chocolate

A bar or box of her favorite chocolate is always the right choice. If you do not know her favorite, a high-quality dark chocolate bar is a safe bet.

Choose a chocolate with a Halloween-themed wrapper if available.

### 12. Halloween Candy Mix

A bag of mixed Halloween candy — candy corn, pumpkin-shaped chocolates, caramel apples — fills the basket and adds a festive touch. Choose a mix that includes her favorites.

Avoid candy she is allergic to — check before buying.

### 13. Spiced Nuts or Popcorn

A bag of spiced nuts or Halloween-flavored popcorn — caramel, cheddar, or kettle corn — is a savory option that balances the sweetness of the candy.

Choose a resealable bag so she can save some for later.

### 14. Hot Chocolate Mix

A jar or packet of premium hot chocolate mix — with marshmallows, or in a pumpkin spice or salted caramel flavor — is a cozy treat that suits the season.

Pair it with the chunky mug for a complete cozy gift set.

### 15. Pumpkin Spice Cookies or Biscuits

A box of pumpkin spice cookies, ginger snaps, or Halloween-shaped shortbread is a festive treat that looks impressive in the basket.

Choose a box with a clear window so the cookies are visible.

## Accessories and Decor

### 16. Halloween Earrings

A pair of small Halloween earrings — ghost studs, pumpkin hoops, or black cat drops — is a fun, wearable gift. Choose a style that suits her everyday jewelry rather than something overtly costume-like.

Sterling silver or gold-filled findings are worth the extra cost for sensitive ears.

### 17. Halloween Tote Bag

A canvas tote bag with a Halloween print — a ghost, a pumpkin, or a spooky quote — is a practical gift she will use for grocery shopping or as a trick-or-treat bag.

Choose a bag with sturdy handles and a flat bottom.

### 18. Small Halloween Decoration

A small ceramic pumpkin, a skull candle holder, or a Halloween-print tea towel is a decorative gift she can use in her home. Choose something in a neutral palette — cream, terracotta, or black — rather than bright orange.

Choose a piece that suits her existing home decor style.

### 19. Halloween Book

A Halloween-themed novel, a cozy mystery, or a book of ghost stories is a thoughtful gift for a reader. Choose a book she has not read rather than a classic she already owns.

Add a bookmark with a Halloween print for extra detail.

### 20. Scented Wax Melts

A set of scented wax melts in fall scents — pumpkin, cinnamon, apple — is a practical gift for someone who already owns a wax warmer. Choose a set with at least three different scents.

Check that she owns a wax warmer before buying wax melts.

## Final Thoughts

The best boo basket is the one that feels personal rather than generic. Start with one item you know she will love — her favorite candle scent, her favorite chocolate, a cozy pair of socks — and build the rest of the basket around it. Presentation matters: a black or orange basket with tissue paper and a handwritten note makes even a modest collection of gifts feel special.
