A funny Halloween costume has one job: make people laugh. The ideas below are chosen for their clarity — each one has a single, readable joke that lands in under three seconds.

Commitment is everything. A funny costume worn with confidence and commitment is always funnier than the same costume worn apologetically.

## Pun Costumes

### 1. Ceiling Fan

A plain outfit with a sign that says "Go Ceilings!" This is one of the most reliably funny Halloween costumes because the pun is unexpected and the setup is so minimal.

Respond to every "What are you?" with enthusiastic ceiling cheering.

### 2. Error 404: Costume Not Found

A plain outfit with a printed sign that says "Error 404: Costume Not Found." This is the laziest Halloween costume on the list, but it is also one of the funniest — especially at a tech-savvy party.

Use a large, readable font on the sign.

### 3. Formal Apology

A formal suit or dress with a sign that says "I'm Sorry." This is a clever pun that works on two levels — the formal outfit and the apology.

Apologize to everyone you meet throughout the evening for extra effect.

### 4. Nudist on Strike

Regular clothes with a sign that says "Nudist on Strike." This is the most minimal funny Halloween costume and one of the most reliably funny.

Use a large, readable font on the sign.

### 5. Deviled Egg

A white outfit with a yellow circle on the front and devil horns. This is a punny, unexpected costume that always gets a reaction.

The yellow circle needs to be large enough to be readable from across the room.

### 6. Holy Guacamole

An avocado costume with a halo. This is a food pun costume that suits someone who enjoys both avocados and wordplay.

The halo is the most important element — without it, it is just an avocado costume.

### 7. Freudian Slip

A slip dress with the word "Freudian" written on it, or a slip dress with Freud's face printed on it. This is a clever, slightly intellectual pun that suits a party with a well-read crowd.

The label needs to be large and readable — the pun only works if people can read it.

### 8. Identity Theft

A plain outfit covered in "Hello My Name Is" stickers with different names on each one. This is a clever, topical pun that suits a party with a tech-savvy or security-conscious crowd.

Use as many name tags as possible — the more, the funnier.

### 9. A Piece of Gum Stuck to the Bottom of a Shoe

A grey outfit with a shoe print on the back. This is an unexpected, specific pun that suits someone who enjoys absurdist humor.

The shoe print needs to be large and clearly visible.

### 10. A Smartie Pants

Trousers covered in Smarties (or M&Ms) with a sign that says "Smartie Pants." This is a sweet, literal pun that suits a family-friendly party.

Glue the Smarties to the trousers with a hot glue gun — they will not stay on with tape.

## Pop Culture Jokes

### 11. Ken (from Barbie)

A pink outfit — pink polo shirt, pink shorts or trousers — with a "Ken" name tag. This is a topical, funny costume that suits a man with a sense of humor about gender expectations.

The more pink, the better.

### 12. Siri or Alexa

A white outfit with a printed logo, a headset, and a sign that says "Ask Me Anything." Respond to every question with a robotic voice and a slightly unhelpful answer.

The robotic voice and the unhelpful answers are what make this costume funny.

### 13. A Netflix Loading Screen

A grey outfit with a spinning loading circle and the Netflix logo. This is a topical, relatable pun that suits a party with a streaming-savvy crowd.

The loading circle needs to be large and clearly visible.

### 14. A Zoom Call

A business outfit on top, pajamas on the bottom, with a sign that says "Zoom Call." This is a relatable, post-pandemic pun that suits almost any party.

The contrast between the top and bottom halves is the joke — make it obvious.

### 15. A Spam Email

A plain outfit covered in printed spam email subject lines — "You've Won a Million Dollars," "Nigerian Prince Needs Your Help," "Enlarge Your Vocabulary." This is a clever, specific pun that suits a tech-savvy crowd.

Use as many subject lines as possible — the more, the funnier.

## Subversive Costumes

### 16. A Responsible Adult

A sensible outfit with a sign that says "Responsible Adult." This is a funny, self-aware costume that suits someone who is known for being the responsible one in their friend group.

The more sensible and boring the outfit, the funnier.

### 17. A Morning Person

A bright, cheerful outfit with a coffee cup and a sign that says "Morning Person." This is a funny, relatable costume that suits someone who is definitively not a morning person.

The contrast between the cheerful costume and the wearer's personality is the joke.

### 18. A Cat Person

A plain outfit with a cat on a leash (a stuffed cat or a cat toy). This is a literal interpretation of "cat person" that suits someone who is known for loving cats.

The stuffed cat needs to be large enough to be recognizable from across the room.

### 19. A Conspiracy Theorist

A plain outfit covered in printed conspiracy theory headlines, connected with red string. Add a tinfoil hat for extra effect.

The tinfoil hat is the most important element — it is the universal symbol of conspiracy theorists.

### 20. A Participation Trophy

A plain outfit with a large "Participation Trophy" ribbon and a sign that says "You Tried." This is a funny, slightly self-deprecating costume that suits someone with a good sense of humor.

The ribbon needs to be large and clearly readable.

## Group Funny Costumes

### 21. Rock, Paper, Scissors

Three people — one dressed as a rock (grey, rounded outfit), one as paper (flat, white outfit with lines), one as scissors (silver outfit with scissor blades). This is a classic punny group costume.

The props are the most important elements — make them large and clearly recognizable.

### 22. A Bag of Jelly Beans

Each person wears a different color outfit and a sign that says their jelly bean flavor. The group wears a large bag-shaped sign together. This is a funny, colorful group costume.

Use large, readable labels — the pun needs to be visible from across the room.

## Final Thoughts

The best funny Halloween costumes are the ones with a single, clear joke that lands immediately. Choose a pun or a concept you find genuinely funny, commit to it completely, and be prepared to explain it to the people who do not get it — that is part of the fun. A costume that makes you laugh is always the right choice.
