Spring is the most rewarding season to decorate for because the changes feel genuinely transformative. After months of heavy textiles and dark colours, even small additions of light, fresh flowers, and softer tones make a home feel completely different.

The ideas below cover every room and every budget, from five-minute changes to more considered updates.

## 1. Swap the Textiles First

The fastest spring decor change is swapping heavy winter cushion covers and throws for lighter ones. Replace wool and velvet with linen, cotton, and light knits in spring colours — soft blush, sage green, pale yellow, or warm cream.

This single change shifts the whole mood of a room before you add a single decoration.

## 2. Bring in Fresh Flowers

A vase of fresh tulips, daffodils, or hyacinths is the most powerful spring decoration available. Place them where they will be seen first — the kitchen counter, the dining table, or the entryway console.

Change the water every two days and cut the stems at an angle to extend their life.

## 3. Add Potted Bulbs

Potted hyacinths, narcissus, and crocuses bring spring inside and fill the room with fragrance. Place them on a windowsill where they will get natural light.

Potted bulbs last two to three weeks and can be planted in the garden afterwards.

## 4. Edit Winter Decor Out

Before adding any spring decorations, remove the heaviest winter elements — dark throws, heavy candles, and any strongly seasonal winter pieces. Replace rather than add.

A room that has been edited feels lighter and more spring-like even before any new pieces are introduced.

## 5. Use a Spring Colour Palette

Choose two or three colours from the spring palette and use them consistently across cushions, flowers, and any decorative objects. Soft blush, sage green, and cream is one of the most versatile spring combinations.

Avoid mixing too many colours — a spring room with a consistent palette always looks more considered.

## 6. Add Greenery and Branches

A vase of budding branches — cherry blossom, forsythia, or pussy willow — creates a dramatic spring display that fills a corner or a mantelpiece.

Cut branches in early spring when the buds are just beginning to swell and place them in a tall vase with fresh water.

## 7. Swap the Doormat

A new doormat in a spring colour or with a floral motif is an inexpensive update that changes the feel of the entrance immediately.

Choose a mat with a rubber backing so it does not slip on wet steps.

## 8. Add a Spring Wreath

Replace the winter wreath with a spring version made from fresh or faux flowers, eucalyptus, or budding branches. A simple greenery wreath with a blush or yellow ribbon suits most door colours.

## 9. Use Lighter Candles

Swap dark, heavily scented winter candles for lighter spring fragrances — fresh linen, green tea, white flowers, or citrus. The fragrance change alone makes a room feel more seasonal.

## 10. Style a Spring Vignette

A small grouping of spring objects on a shelf, console, or coffee table — a vase of flowers, a ceramic bird, a small potted plant, and a pastel candle — creates a contained spring moment on any surface.

## 11. Add a Spring Table Runner

A table runner in a floral print, a soft stripe, or a natural linen with a spring colour adds a seasonal note to the dining table without requiring a full table setting change.

## 12. Open the Windows

The simplest spring decor change costs nothing. Open the windows, let in the light and the fresh air, and let the season do the decorating.

## Final Thoughts

Spring decorating is about lightness — lighter textiles, lighter colours, lighter fragrances, and more natural light. Start with the textiles and fresh flowers, edit out the heaviest winter pieces, and add spring accents gradually. The result should feel like the season itself: fresh, bright, and genuinely welcoming.
