A pumpkin baby shower is one of the warmest and most charming baby shower themes. It suits any gender, works beautifully in autumn, and creates a celebration that feels genuinely seasonal and genuinely personal.

The ideas below cover decorations, food, games, and favours for a pumpkin baby shower that feels warm, whimsical, and beautifully styled.

## 1. Choose a Soft, Warm Palette

The most important decision for a pumpkin baby shower is the colour palette. Avoid bright orange — it reads as Halloween rather than baby shower.

Choose a soft, warm palette instead: cream, rust, and sage green; cream, peach, and gold; or neutral cream, terracotta, and natural linen.

## 2. Use the Theme Phrase Consistently

Choose one theme phrase and use it consistently across invitations, signage, and decorations. The most popular options are:

- "Little Pumpkin"
- "Our Little Pumpkin"
- "A Pumpkin is on the Way"
- "The Cutest Pumpkin in the Patch"

## 3. Create a Pumpkin Patch Photo Backdrop

A backdrop of real or faux pumpkins in varying sizes, with greenery and fairy lights, creates a beautiful photo opportunity. Add a sign with the theme phrase for a complete backdrop.

Use cream, white, and rust pumpkins rather than bright orange for the most photogenic result.

## 4. Style the Table With Pumpkins and Greenery

A table styled with small pumpkins, gourds, dried leaves, and candles in the shower's colour palette creates a warm, seasonal centerpiece.

Use a garland runner of eucalyptus and dried leaves with pumpkins and candles placed among it for the most impactful table display.

## 5. Serve Pumpkin-Themed Food

A pumpkin baby shower menu might include pumpkin spice cupcakes, pumpkin bread, pumpkin soup, pumpkin pie, and pumpkin spice latte.

Label all food clearly and provide non-caffeinated options for the mother-to-be.

## 6. Make a Pumpkin Cake

A pumpkin-shaped cake or a cake decorated with pumpkin imagery is the centrepiece of the food display. Choose a design that suits the shower's colour palette.

A simple naked cake with cream cheese frosting and a small pumpkin on top is one of the most popular options.

## 7. Play Pumpkin-Themed Games

Adapt classic baby shower games for the pumpkin theme. "Guess the Pumpkin's Weight" uses a real pumpkin instead of the mother-to-be's bump. "Pumpkin Decorating" gives each guest a small pumpkin to decorate as a baby. "Pumpkin Patch Bingo" uses pumpkin imagery on the bingo cards.

## 8. Give Personalised Pumpkin Favours

Small personalised pumpkins with the baby's name and due date written on them are the most popular pumpkin baby shower favour. Guests can display them as seasonal decorations.

Other popular options include pumpkin-shaped candles, small jars of pumpkin butter, and pumpkin seed packets.

## 9. Create a Pumpkin Nappy Cake

A nappy cake decorated with pumpkin ribbon, small pumpkin ornaments, and a pumpkin on top is a practical gift that also serves as a decoration.

## 10. Add a Gratitude Pumpkin

Place a large white pumpkin at the entrance with markers and ask guests to write a wish or message for the baby on the pumpkin. The mother-to-be can keep it as a keepsake.

## 11. Use Pumpkin-Shaped Balloons

Pumpkin-shaped balloons in cream, rust, and gold add a whimsical touch to the decoration without overwhelming the space.

Use them sparingly — two or three large pumpkin balloons are more effective than a room full of them.

## 12. Include a Book Instead of a Card

Ask guests to bring a children's book instead of a card, with a message written inside. This builds the baby's library and creates a collection of personal messages.

## Final Thoughts

A pumpkin baby shower is most successful when the palette is soft and warm rather than bright orange, the theme phrase is used consistently, and the food and favours reflect the theme without being overwhelming. The mother-to-be and her guests will remember the warmth of the occasion long after the pumpkins have been composted.
