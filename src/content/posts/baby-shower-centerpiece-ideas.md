A baby shower centerpiece sets the tone for the celebration table and creates the visual atmosphere that guests will remember. The best baby shower centerpieces are personal, beautiful, and low enough to allow conversation across the table.

The ideas below cover every style and budget.

## 1. The Fresh Flower Arrangement

A generous arrangement of fresh flowers in the shower's colour palette — blush roses, white peonies, or yellow sunflowers — in a clear glass vase is the most versatile baby shower centerpiece.

Use a mix of focal flowers, secondary flowers, and greenery for the most interesting arrangement.

## 2. The Nappy Cake

A nappy cake — rolled nappies arranged in tiers and decorated with ribbon, small baby items, and themed decorations — is both a beautiful centerpiece and a practical gift.

Decorate it with items the parents will actually use — a soft toy, a muslin cloth, and a small bottle of baby lotion.

## 3. The Balloon Centerpiece

A cluster of balloons in the shower's colour palette, tied to a weight and placed in the centre of the table, creates a festive, celebratory centerpiece.

Use a mix of latex and foil balloons in two or three colours from the palette.

## 4. The Bud Vase Collection

A collection of small bud vases in different heights, each holding a single stem or a small bunch of flowers, creates a relaxed, gathered look.

Use vases in the same colour or material for a cohesive result.

## 5. The Themed Display

A display of baby items — a pair of tiny shoes, a soft toy, and a small book — arranged on a wooden board with flowers and greenery creates a personal, themed centerpiece.

## 6. The Candle and Flower Display

A grouping of pillar candles at varying heights, surrounded by loose flower heads and greenery, creates a romantic, intimate centerpiece.

## 7. The Terrarium Display

A glass terrarium filled with flowers, greenery, and a small baby figurine creates a contained, modern centerpiece.

## 8. The Fruit and Flower Display

A wide bowl filled with seasonal fruit — strawberries, grapes, and melon — with flowers tucked around them creates a colourful, practical centerpiece.

## 9. The Pampas Grass Arrangement

A loose arrangement of pampas grass, dried flowers, and eucalyptus in a terracotta pot creates a boho, modern centerpiece that suits a neutral baby shower.

## 10. The Personalised Centerpiece

A centerpiece that incorporates the baby's name — on a small sign, a balloon, or a personalised ornament — makes the shower feel genuinely special.

## Final Thoughts

The best baby shower centerpieces are personal, beautiful, and low enough to allow conversation. Start with fresh flowers in the shower's colour palette, add one personal element, and keep the arrangement low. The result should feel genuinely celebratory and genuinely welcoming.
