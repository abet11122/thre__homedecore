A baby shower table is the visual centrepiece of the celebration. A beautifully set table — with the right centerpiece, warm lighting, and personal touches — creates the atmosphere that guests will remember and photograph.

The ideas below cover every element of baby shower table decor.

## 1. The Table Runner

A table runner in the shower's colour palette sets the tone for the whole table. Use a linen runner for a neutral or boho shower, a floral print for a garden shower, or a themed fabric for a character-based shower.

## 2. The Centerpiece

A fresh flower arrangement, a balloon centerpiece, or a themed display creates the visual focal point of the table.

See the [baby shower centerpiece ideas](/post/baby-shower-centerpiece-ideas/) post for a full guide.

## 3. The Place Setting

A plate on a charger or placemat, a folded napkin, and a small favour at each place creates a layered, considered place setting.

## 4. The Favour

A small favour at each place setting — a personalised candle, a small jar of honey, or a themed treat — doubles as a decoration and a thank-you gift.

## 5. The Food Table

The food table should be styled with tiered stands, fresh flowers or themed decorations, matching serving dishes, and labels for each food item.

Add a sign with the baby's name or a baby shower message above the table.

## 6. The Drinks Station

A styled drinks station with matching glasses, a pitcher of punch or lemonade, and a few flowers or themed decorations creates a welcoming focal point.

## 7. Balloons

A balloon garland, a balloon arch, or a cluster of balloons in the shower's colour palette adds a festive, celebratory atmosphere.

## 8. Fresh Flowers

Fresh flowers on the dining table, the food table, and the drinks station create a generous, celebratory atmosphere.

## 9. Candles or Fairy Lights

Candles or fairy lights add warmth and romance to the baby shower table. Use them on the dining table and the food table.

## 10. A Sign or Banner

A "Baby Shower" banner, a sign with the baby's name, or a personalised message above the food table adds a celebratory note.

## Final Thoughts

A beautiful baby shower table comes from a consistent colour palette, fresh flowers, and a few personal touches at each place. Start with the runner and the centerpiece, set the places, and add candles and flowers throughout. The result should feel genuinely celebratory and genuinely welcoming.
