Dark hair is an asset for Halloween costumes. Many of the most iconic, most striking, and most recognizable Halloween characters are canonically brunette — which means you can wear them without a wig and look more authentic than someone who has to cover their natural hair.

The ideas below are organized by style. All of them work with dark brown or black hair.

## Gothic and Witchy

### 1. Wednesday Addams

A black dress with a white collar, two braids, and a deadpan expression. Wednesday has dark hair — this is one of the most authentic brunette Halloween costumes. The key is the expression: Wednesday never smiles.

A black dress with a white Peter Pan collar is the most accurate version.

### 2. Morticia Addams

A long black dress, pale skin, dark lips, and long dark hair worn loose. Morticia's long, dark hair is one of her most defining features. This is an elegant, sophisticated costume that suits a brunette perfectly.

A long, form-fitting black dress is the most important piece.

### 3. The Wicked Witch of the West

A black dress, a pointed hat, green face paint, and dark hair. The Wicked Witch has dark hair — this is an authentic brunette costume. The green face paint is the most important element.

Use theatrical face paint rather than craft paint for the green skin.

### 4. Maleficent

A black dress, dramatic horns, pale makeup, dark lips, and dark hair. Maleficent's dark hair is one of her most defining features. This is a striking, dramatic costume.

The horns are the most important piece — choose a quality pair that stays on securely.

### 5. The Evil Queen (Snow White)

A black and purple gown, a crown, pale makeup, dark lips, and dark hair. The Evil Queen has dark hair — this is an authentic brunette costume.

The crown and the dark lips are the two most important elements.

## Disney and Animated

### 6. Snow White

A yellow and blue dress, a red headband, pale skin, red lips, and dark hair in a short bob. Snow White is one of the most iconic brunette Disney princesses. The short dark bob is her most defining feature.

A red headband and red lips are the two most important elements.

### 7. Moana

A teal and red outfit with a flower in the hair and long, dark, wavy hair. Moana's long dark hair is one of her most defining features. This is a colorful, recognizable costume.

The flower in the hair and the teal outfit are the two most important elements.

### 8. Belle (Beauty and the Beast)

A yellow ball gown or a blue and white village dress, with dark hair in a half-up style. Belle has dark hair — this is an authentic brunette Disney princess costume.

The yellow ball gown is the most recognizable version.

### 9. Esmeralda (The Hunchback of Notre Dame)

A white off-shoulder top, a green skirt, gold jewelry, and long dark hair. Esmeralda has dark hair — this is an authentic brunette Disney character costume.

The gold jewelry and the green skirt are the two most important elements.

### 10. Megara (Hercules)

A purple dress, a high ponytail with a purple ribbon, and a knowing expression. Megara has dark hair — this is an authentic brunette Disney character costume.

The high ponytail with the purple ribbon is the most important element.

## Pop Culture

### 11. Hermione Granger

A Gryffindor robe, round glasses, a wand, and bushy dark hair. Hermione has dark hair — this is an authentic brunette pop culture costume. The bushy hair is her most defining feature.

The Gryffindor robe and the wand are the two most important elements.

### 12. Katniss Everdeen (The Hunger Games)

A dark outfit, a braid over one shoulder, a bow and arrow prop, and dark hair. Katniss has dark hair — this is an authentic brunette pop culture costume.

The braid and the bow and arrow prop are the two most important elements.

### 13. Lara Croft (Tomb Raider)

A tank top, cargo shorts, boots, a braid, and dark hair. Lara Croft has dark hair — this is an authentic brunette pop culture costume.

The braid and the cargo shorts are the two most important elements.

### 14. Mia Wallace (Pulp Fiction)

A white shirt, black trousers, a black bob, and a bloody nose. Mia Wallace has dark hair in a bob — this is an authentic brunette pop culture costume.

The black bob and the bloody nose are the two most important elements.

### 15. Eleven (Stranger Things — Season 1)

A pink dress, a blonde wig — or, for a brunette, the Season 2 version with dark, curly hair and a black outfit. The Season 2 version is more authentic for a brunette.

The dark, curly hair and the black outfit are the most important elements for the Season 2 version.

## Historical and Mythological

### 16. Cleopatra

A white or gold dress, a black wig with a blunt fringe — or, for a brunette, dark hair styled with a headpiece. Dramatic eye makeup and gold jewelry complete the look.

The dramatic eye makeup is the most important element.

### 17. Pocahontas

A tan suede dress, a feather in the hair, and long dark hair. Pocahontas has long dark hair — this is an authentic brunette costume.

The feather in the hair and the tan suede dress are the two most important elements.

### 18. Medusa

A green dress, snakes woven into the hair, and dramatic makeup. Medusa's hair is famously full of snakes — dark hair makes the snakes more visible and more striking.

Fake snakes woven into a bun or a braid create the most convincing Medusa hair.

### 19. Frida Kahlo

A colorful embroidered blouse, a long skirt, flowers in the hair, and drawn-on connected eyebrows. Frida Kahlo had dark hair — this is an authentic brunette costume.

The flowers in the hair and the connected eyebrows are the two most important elements.

### 20. Scheherazade (One Thousand and One Nights)

A flowing, jewel-toned outfit with gold jewelry, a veil, and long dark hair. Scheherazade is a literary character with dark hair — this is an authentic brunette costume.

The jewel-toned outfit and the gold jewelry are the two most important elements.

## Final Thoughts

Dark hair is one of the most versatile assets for Halloween costumes. Many of the most iconic, most striking, and most recognizable Halloween characters are canonically brunette — which means you can wear them without a wig and look more authentic than someone who has to cover their natural hair. Start with the characters you love, choose the one whose look you can execute best, and commit to the costume completely.
