A garden bridal shower is one of the most beautiful ways to celebrate a bride-to-be. The natural setting provides a ready-made backdrop of flowers, greenery, and natural light — and the relaxed, outdoor atmosphere creates a genuinely joyful celebration.

The ideas below cover every element of a garden bridal shower, from the decorations to the food.

## 1. Work With the Garden

The garden itself is the most beautiful decoration available. Position the tables to take advantage of the best views, use existing flower beds as backdrops, and let the natural light do the work.

Avoid covering the garden with so many decorations that the natural beauty is lost.

## 2. Add String Lights

String lights hung between trees, along a fence, or from a temporary structure add a magical quality to a garden bridal shower, especially for an afternoon event that extends into the evening.

Use warm-white lights for the most romantic effect.

## 3. Create a Flower Crown Station

A flower crown station — a table with fresh flowers, greenery, and wire for guests to make their own flower crowns — is one of the most popular garden bridal shower activities.

Provide instructions and a mirror so guests can see the result.

## 4. Set Beautiful Tables

Set the tables with linen runners, fresh flower centerpieces, cloth napkins, and a small favour at each place.

Use mismatched vintage crockery for a relaxed, garden party feel, or matching white plates for a more formal look.

## 5. Create a Photo Backdrop

A flower wall, a fabric drape between two trees, or a natural garden feature — a rose arch, a hedge, or a pergola — creates a beautiful photo backdrop.

Add a sign with the bride's name or a bridal shower message for a personalised touch.

## 6. Add Lanterns and Candles

Lanterns placed on tables and along paths add warmth and romance. Use LED candles for outdoor use where wind is a factor.

## 7. Serve a Garden Party Menu

Finger sandwiches, scones, fresh fruit, macarons, and a tiered cake suit the garden bridal shower setting perfectly.

Set up a prosecco or elderflower cordial drinks station with fresh fruit and flowers as garnishes.

## 8. Play Garden Games

Croquet, lawn bowls, or a flower arranging competition add an interactive element that guests enjoy.

## 9. Add a Flower Arrangement Activity

Provide each guest with a small vase and a selection of flowers and greenery to arrange. The finished arrangements can be taken home as favours.

## 10. Have a Weather Contingency

Always have a weather contingency plan — a marquee, a gazebo, or an indoor backup. Inform guests in advance of the contingency plan so they know what to expect.

## Final Thoughts

A garden bridal shower is most beautiful when it works with the natural setting rather than trying to transform it. Start with the garden's existing features, add string lights and fresh flowers, set beautiful tables, and include one interactive activity. The result should feel genuinely celebratory and genuinely connected to the outdoors.
